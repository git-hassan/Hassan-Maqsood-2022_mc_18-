Lab 6 - Observations
====================
Student: Hassan Maqsood (2022-MC-18)
Course: MCT-454L Mobile Robotics

1. Robot Behavior Near Obstacles
----------------------------------
- The robot successfully detected obstacles using front LiDAR region
- When front distance dropped below 0.5m threshold, the robot stopped immediately
- The robot then turned toward the side with greater clearance (left or right)
- Response was consistent and reliable across multiple obstacle encounters

2. Oscillations or Instability
--------------------------------
- Minor oscillations observed during wall following due to proportional control
- Tuning the gain (0.3) helped reduce overshoot while maintaining wall distance
- At sharp corners the robot occasionally oscillated before stabilizing
- Averaging over 30 front readings (15 on each side of 0°) reduced noise significantly

3. Effect of Changing Threshold Values
----------------------------------------
- front_threshold = 0.5m: robot stopped with safe margin before obstacle
- Reducing to 0.3m: robot got closer before stopping, riskier near fast turns
- Increasing to 0.7m: robot stopped too early, inefficient in narrow corridors
- side_threshold = 0.4m: adequate for corridor navigation without wall collision
